# Reflection — Feature Plan Portfolio (DRAFT, edit before submitting)

> This draft was written from the decisions made while drafting the plans with AI. Swap in your own experience and your team's wording before you turn it in.

The hardest feature to make testable was campaign chat (F9). "Players can chat" isn't testable on its own, so we had to choose polling over sockets, set a 5-second interval, and write acceptance criteria for timing, deleted messages and pausing in a background tab. AI drafts kept adding scope we had to cut: password reset by email, image uploads, real-time WebSockets, initiative and HP tracking in encounters, and the spec's game systems, items and maps, which aren't on our whiteboard. The dependency that changed our order was role-based access. At first NPCs came right after campaigns, but every content feature needs the GM/Player and "revealed" rules, so we moved players and access (F5) ahead of NPCs, notes, encounters and chat. Planning also exposed duplicated data in our whiteboard model: User `gmId`/`pcId` arrays and Campaign `npcsId`/`campaignId` would have drifted out of sync, so we store each fact once and query for the rest. It also showed that players would see every NPC unless we add an `isRevealed` flag, which the team still has to approve before F6 starts.
